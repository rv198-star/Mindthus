## ROI Beta exception

`v1.8.0` intentionally does not publish `v1.8.0-roi-beta`.

This exception was recorded before publication in README, CHANGELOG, and `docs/releases/v1.8.0.md`. Root-Cause Replacement changes the `using-mindthus` / Anti-Spiral recovery surface that ROI.2 overlays with a previously qualified thin entry. Republishing Beta without updating and requalifying that overlay would overstate behavioral inheritance.

Current ROI Beta remains `v1.7.1-roi-beta`. No `v1.8.0-roi-beta` tag or asset was created.
