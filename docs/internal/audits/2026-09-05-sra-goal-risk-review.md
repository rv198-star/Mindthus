# SRA goal alignment — final risk review

Date: 2026-09-05

Authorization: the maintainer approved goal alignment, a final risk review, then bounded implementation. This is not independent-model certification.

## Decision

Proceed with corrections below. SRA allocates scarce resources to work under the user's objective, time horizon and protected constraints. It first protects what makes that objective viable, then compares the risk-adjusted marginal value of meaningful additional commitments. Analysis itself spends the same scarce resources.

“Survive, then strengthen” is a mnemonic for viability before optional improvement, not a license to substitute bare survival for the user's goal or to rank the worth of people.

## Risk challenges and adopted corrections

| Challenge | Failure to prevent | Adopted rule |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue versus safety, dignity or commitments | A favorable ratio buys permission to violate a protected boundary | Establish authorized constraints before comparing feasible uses. SRA ranks work, not human worth. Ambiguous authority needs clarification, not a second model pretending to grant permission. |
| Measurable quick wins versus maintenance, learning or care | Unknown or delayed value becomes zero; visible metrics replace the actual objective | Compare the declared horizon and affected parties; preserve qualitative and unknown value. Use bounded exploration or partial orders where justified. No compulsory numeric ROI score. |
| High expected gain with unacceptable downside | Averaging masks ruin, concentration or irreversible harm | Reject breaches of the risk floor; size reversible commitments, consider shared downside and reserve. High consequence still requires calibration even when the task list is short. |
| Survival rhetoric | Every incumbent item becomes permanently necessary | Contraction holds the real target fixed, tests minimum sufficient support and stops investing at its threshold. Already-spent resources confer no priority. |
| A tiny high-ratio action versus an indivisible useful bundle | Greedy ranking funds fragments that never produce an outcome | Compare meaningful tranches and complementary bundles against actual capacity, dependencies and the window. |
| Method ritual | A small reversible choice pays for complete packets, several models and verbose audit prose | Direct execution is valid. Conversational Lite needs the floor, next useful commitment and a reopen trigger; controlled artifacts use the same rigorous runtime, only when warranted. Stop analysis when its incremental benefit is unlikely to repay its cost. |
| Short action with large harm | “The action takes seconds” is used to skip safety | Budget analysis against avoidable error consequences, not action duration alone. |

These are adversarial design cases assessed by the reviewer. They are not measured real-use improvements, model A/B results or evidence of optimal allocation.

## Corrections to the earlier optimization proposals

1. Full versus Lite describes required reasoning depth. Separate calibration policy is reasonable, but absence of contamination alone is insufficient to reduce checking. New proportionate policy must explicitly assess reversibility/consequence; uncertainty and high consequence retain dual views. Coverage and authority stay separate.
2. Do not silently reinterpret existing v0.3 runs. A named, input-hashed policy extension in v0.4 input selects the new behavior; legacy v0.3 input retains the conservative policy and deterministic replay. A forward-compatibility probe found the old parser ignores unknown v0.3 fields, so an explicit new input version is required to make old programs refuse new policies.
3. An intake helper may generate IDs, defaults and draft shape. It cannot invent goals, capacity, evidence, dependency satisfaction, necessity or permission. Unknown required semantics remain incomplete; a draft is not an allocation.
4. A short result is a projection of a checked final decision, never a second source. Protected constraints, resource bounds, start conditions and blocking status remain visible.
5. Completion references may remove textual false conflicts only when they bind a task-appropriate, sourced, immutable criterion. They do not prove the criterion is sufficient. Free text without a reference remains conservatively compared.
6. Re-ranking creates a fresh draft with refreshed inputs; prior judgment is traceable background, never current authority. No Mission mutation or autonomous recurring execution.

## Implementation boundary

No employee scoring, universal utility function, mandatory numeric quota, new global router, release/tag, history deletion, TPlan schema change or host authorization changes. Existing resource/demand/dependency/repair protections remain required regression owners. Product outcome claims remain evidence-bounded.
